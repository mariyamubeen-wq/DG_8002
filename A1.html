A Guide to Spell Casting in Pathfinder 2ed

Playing a spell caster in Pathfinder 2 edition (will be referred to as PF2e) can be confusing as a new player. It is also confusing as an avid player. Here’s a small guide on what different categories and words mean to help navigate spell casting in the game.

Spells

First let's talk about spells. In PF2e, spells are divided into 4 spell lists or spell schools. Each spell list is also associated with a skill. The spell lists are as follows with their respective skills:

  • Divine - Religion
  • Primal - Nature
  • Arcane - Arcana
  • Occult - Occultism

Types of spells

Spells usually require a spell slot to cast. Different casters have a different pool of spell slots that vary as they level up. Spells are divided into 4 types based on the kind of spell slot they use:

  • Cantrips

    Simple spells that don’t require a spell slot to cast. They are the most basic spells that level up with your character.

  • Spells

    These are more taxing spells that require a spell slot to cast. Spells have ranks and so do spell slots. As a caster levels up they get access to higher spell slots and better spells. Casters have a limited amount of spell slots for each rank and once the player has used their spell slots they can no longer cast spells. Spell slots renew after a long rest.

  • Focus Spells

    Class specific spells that are associated with a type of study or deity. They are obtained usually as part of a class and are different from regular spells. Focus spells also have different spell slots that are called a Focus Pool. A character can only have a maximum of 3 slots in their pool. These slots renew after 10 minutes of refocusing time.

  • Rituals

    These are more taxing spells that require a spell slot to cast. Spells have ranks and so do spell slots. As a caster levels up they get access to higher spell slots and better spells. Casters have a limited amount of spell slots for each rank and once the player has used their spell slots they can no longer cast spells. Spell slots renew after a long rest.

Spell Casters

Classes in PF2e that use spells are called casters. Some classes are full casters, i.e their entire kit revolves around casting spells to heal/help allies or hurt/hinder enemies. Other classes are semi-casters, meaning their abilities do not fully rely on spells. Instead, they could also have some combat ability that they infuse magic into to complete their kit or a companion that is infused with magic.
Spell casters also depend on certain attributes that determine their strength with their magic called their Key Attributes. Different Classes have different Key Attributes. The following table is a quick cheat sheet on different caster type classes:

Psychic Intelligence or Charisma Full Cantrip, Spells, Focus
